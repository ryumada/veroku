VEROKU — Legal Policies

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately

🔒 100% Client-Side & Privacy-First

1. Privacy Policy

Veroku ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. Veroku is designed from the ground up as an offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA). We do not operate external application tracking servers, databases, or analytics engines.

1.1 Information We Collect & How It Is Stored

  • Vehicle & Maintenance Logs: All odometer readings, parts tracker intervals, maintenance history records, checklist tasks, and notes entered into Veroku remain strictly on your device inside your browser's local storage (localStorage).
  • No Server Telemetry: We do not track, collect, transmit, or log your personal usage, IP addresses, location data, or vehicle information.

1.2 Google Drive Cloud Backup (Google OAuth & Data Access)

Veroku provides an optional feature allowing users to back up and restore their vehicle data using their Google account:

  • Google Identity & Profile (openid, userinfo.profile, userinfo.email): We request basic profile info solely to display your name, email, and avatar in the Veroku settings bar to indicate that you are connected. This information is held temporarily in your browser session and is never sent to any third party.
  • Google Drive AppData Access (drive.appdata): Veroku uses the Google Drive API strictly with the isolated drive.appdata scope. This sandboxed permission gives Veroku access only to its own hidden configuration file (veroku-backup.json). Veroku cannot read, view, modify, or access any personal files, documents, photos, or spreadsheets stored in your Google Drive.
  • Zero Data Sharing: Your backup data is transferred directly and securely between your browser and Google's official API servers. We do not intercept, store, sell, or analyze your backup files.

1.3 Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can completely and permanently delete all local vehicle data at any time via Settings → Danger Zone → Delete All Data.
  • Cloud Data: You can disconnect your Google account at any time via Settings → Google Drive Cloud Backup → Disconnect, which immediately revokes the active OAuth token. You can delete the cloud backup file via Google Drive's "Manage Apps" settings or through Veroku.
